01 / about

About

I turn business processes into AI agents that people actually use, and I teach others to build them.

I work in the Modern Workplace and Microsoft team at PwC Luxembourg, where I design agentic solutions with Copilot Studio, Copilot Cowork, Power Automate and SharePoint. My approach starts from the process: I map it in BPMN, find where an agent adds value, build it, test it with users and drive adoption.

I have delivered Microsoft 365 and Copilot programmes across banking, tax, insurance and the public sector, from PMO and target operating models to licence-allocation workshops and governance frameworks.

PwC GenAI Champion since 2024 and lead instructor of a Copilot upskilling programme for about 400 consultants. I work in Italian, English and French.

  • ~400 consultants in the Copilot upskilling programme
  • 19 Copilot licence-allocation workshops with business lines
  • 2024 PwC GenAI Champion since
  • 3 working languages: Italian, English, French
