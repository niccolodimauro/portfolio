Profile

I design agentic AI solutions that are anchored in real processes, governed from day one and adopted by the business.

I am a Senior Associate in the Modern Workplace and Microsoft team at PwC Luxembourg. My work covers the full delivery cycle of AI solutions on Copilot Studio, Copilot Cowork, Power Automate and SharePoint: process analysis and BPMN modelling, solution design, build, user acceptance testing and adoption.

Alongside AI delivery, I support Microsoft 365 and Copilot programmes for clients in banking, tax, insurance and the public sector, including programme management, target operating models, licence allocation and governance frameworks.

As a PwC GenAI Champion since 2024, I lead the firm's Copilot upskilling programme for around 400 consultants, advise leadership on use-case adoption and present AI solutions at client and industry events. I work in English, French and Italian.
